# Competitive Landscape

The original brief for this asked for "15+ companies mapped." That wasn't done. Here's why: the choice was between padding this list with names half-remembered and never checked, or giving a shorter list of companies actually verified as real and active, with real technology. Went with the second one. The padded version would just be a different, worse document.

Four companies below, each checked directly against their own site. Not a search engine; WebSearch was out of budget for this session, so this was done by fetching each company's site directly.

## Verified competitors

### 374Water: AirSCWO (supercritical water oxidation)
Destroys PFAS by converting organic waste, including PFAS, into minerals and clean water using supercritical water oxidation. Claims up to 99.9999% contamination reduction. Multiple commercial-scale systems running for industrial, federal, and municipal clients (AFFF/rinse water, GAC, ion exchange resin, biosolids, landfill leachate).

**Approach:** physical/chemical destruction, not biological.

### Cyclopure: DEXSORB®
A "renewable adsorbent" for pulling PFAS out of drinking water. Sold as countertop and whole-house filters (Purefast® line) plus commercial-scale systems, and their own PFAS test kit.

**Approach:** adsorption/filtration, not destruction, not biological.

### Claros Technologies: ClarosTechUV™
Claims to destroy 99.99% of long, short, and ultra-short chain PFAS. The actual underlying mechanism isn't disclosed on their public site beyond "proprietary" and "science-driven." The "UV" in the product name suggests ultraviolet-based treatment, though that couldn't be confirmed directly. Also runs ClarosLabs™, an accredited PFAS detection/testing service (3.8M+ liters treated to date, per their site).

**Approach:** unclear/undisclosed, likely UV-based destruction. Not biological as far as could be verified.

### Allonnia: SAFF® (Surface Active Foam Fractionation)
Allonnia's actual core business is mining biotech: extracting more metal from existing mines via engineered microbes. PFAS treatment is a secondary product line, not their main focus. SAFF® concentrates PFAS out of water using foam fractionation.

**Approach:** physical concentration, not destruction. Notably, this is the one company on this list that's genuinely a biotech company (engineered microbes for mining), but that capability isn't being applied to their PFAS product.

## The actual gap

None of these four use AI-driven or computational enzyme design. None of them are attacking PFAS via engineered biological catalysis the way this project's pipeline does (RFdiffusion → ProteinMPNN → ESMFold, scaffolding real catalytic chemistry from a natural C-F bond-cleaving enzyme). They're doing physical concentration, physical/chemical destruction, or adsorption: proven and deployable, but fundamentally different from designing a protein that does the chemistry.

**Raw note, stated honestly:** four verified companies is not a competitive landscape. It's a start. There may well be AI-enzyme-design PFAS companies that simply weren't found here; a proper search would need WebSearch budget that wasn't available this session, or a literature/patent search specifically for "computational PFAS enzyme design" companies rather than "PFAS remediation" companies generally. Treat this section as incomplete, not as proof no one else is doing this.
